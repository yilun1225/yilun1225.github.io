About me

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Columbia Business School, where I was hosted by Yash Kanoria. I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research from Cornell University under the supervision of David Goldberg, and my B.S. in Mathematics from Yuanpei College, Peking University.

Research interests

My research is in Operations Research, particularly stochastic modeling. I often find myself drawn to problems at the intersection of economics, theoretical computer science, and statistics, where uncertainty, incentives, and algorithmic decisions interact. My work spans sequential decision-making under uncertainty, online resource allocation, dynamic matching, and algorithmic/mechanism design, often with applications to platforms and marketplaces.

  • Sequential decisions
  • Resource allocation
  • Dynamic matching
  • Mechanism design
